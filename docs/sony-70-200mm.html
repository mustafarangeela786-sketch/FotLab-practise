Sony FE 70-200mm f/2.8 GM OSS II

Brand: Sony   |   Category: G Master Telephoto Zoom
Back to Shop
Product Description
The Sony FE 70-200mm f/2.8 GM OSS II is the second generation of Sony's flagship telephoto zoom lens, and it improves on its already outstanding predecessor in nearly every way. With a constant f/2.8 aperture, four XD (Extreme Dynamic) Linear Motors for lightning-fast autofocus, and Optical SteadyShot stabilization, this lens excels at sports, wildlife, events, and portrait photography. Weighing significantly less than the original GM version, it is also more balanced on Sony mirrorless bodies. The G Master optics deliver Sony's hallmark combination of edge-to-edge sharpness and silky smooth bokeh.
Key Features
  • Constant f/2.8 Aperture Throughout Zoom Range
  • 4 XD Linear AF Motors — Extremely Fast and Silent
  • Optical SteadyShot (OSS) Image Stabilization
  • G Master Optics: Edge-to-Edge Sharpness
  • Fluorine Front Element Coating (Easy to Clean)
  • Weather-Sealed Body (Dust and Moisture Resistant)
  • Customizable Focus Hold and AF/MF Switch Buttons
  • Removable, Rotating Tripod Collar Included
  • 11-Blade Circular Aperture for Natural Bokeh
  • Built-in 1.4x Teleconverter Compatible (with TC)
  • Compatible with All Sony E-Mount Cameras
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 0.4m (Wide) / 0.82m (Tele)
