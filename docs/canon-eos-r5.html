Product Description
The Canon EOS R5 is a professional-grade mirrorless camera built for photographers and videographers who demand the absolute best. Featuring a 45-megapixel full-frame CMOS sensor, it delivers extraordinary image quality with exceptional detail. Its ability to record internal 8K RAW video makes it one of the most capable hybrid cameras ever made. Backed by Dual Pixel CMOS AF II and an advanced in-body image stabilization system, the EOS R5 performs brilliantly in every shooting scenario — from fast-action sports to cinematic video production.
Key Features
  • 45MP Full-Frame CMOS Sensor
  • 8K RAW Internal Video Recording
  • Dual Pixel CMOS AF II with Deep Learning
  • 20fps Continuous Shooting (Electronic Shutter)
  • 5-Axis In-Body Image Stabilization (IBIS)
  • Animal and Human Eye Detection AF
  • Dual Card Slots (CFexpress + SD UHS-II)
  • Weather-Sealed Magnesium Alloy Body
  • 0.5-inch OLED Electronic Viewfinder (5.76M dot)
  • 3.15-inch Fully Articulating Touch Screen
  • Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and USB-C Connectivity
  • RF Lens Mount — Compatible with RF and EF Lenses
