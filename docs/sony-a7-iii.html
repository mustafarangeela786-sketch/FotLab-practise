Sony A7 III

Brand: Sony   |   Category: Full-Frame Mirrorless

Product Description
The Sony A7 III is widely considered the best all-around full-frame mirrorless camera in its class. Built around a 24.2-megapixel back-illuminated Exmor R CMOS sensor and Sony's BIONZ X image processor, it delivers outstanding image quality across a wide range of lighting conditions. With 693 phase-detection AF points covering 93% of the frame, a 10fps burst speed, and 5-axis optical steady shot stabilization, the A7 III is a true workhorse for wedding photographers, portrait artists, landscape shooters, and videographers alike. Its 4K video with full pixel readout and no crop makes it a compelling option for hybrid shooters.
Key Features
  • 24.2MP Back-Illuminated Exmor R CMOS Sensor
  • 693 Phase-Detection AF Points (93% Frame Coverage)
  • 10fps Continuous Shooting with AF/AE
  • 4K Video with Full Pixel Readout (No Crop)
  • 5-Axis Optical SteadyShot IBIS (5 stops)
  • Real-Time Eye AF and Animal Eye AF
  • Silent Shooting Mode (Electronic Shutter)
  • Dual SD Card Slots (UHS-II + UHS-I)
  • Weather-Sealed Body (Dust and Moisture Resistant)
  • 3.0-inch Tilting Touchscreen (921K Dots)
  • 0.78x OLED Viewfinder (2.36M Dots)
  • USB-C Charging, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC
