Product Description
The Canon RF 24-70mm f/2.8 L IS USM is a flagship standard zoom lens designed for Canon's RF mirrorless system. It covers the most essential focal range for photographers — from wide-angle landscapes at 24mm to flattering portrait compression at 70mm — all with a constant f/2.8 maximum aperture. Built to L-series standards with weather sealing and featuring Canon's Dual Sensing IS (Image Stabilizer) alongside IBIS-capable bodies, this lens delivers up to 7.5 stops of combined stabilization. The ring-type USM autofocus motor provides near-silent, fast, and accurate focusing for both stills and video.
Key Features
  • Constant f/2.8 Maximum Aperture Throughout Zoom Range
  • Dual Sensing Image Stabilization (IS)
  • Up to 7.5 Stops Shake Correction (with IBIS body)
  • Ring-Type USM for Fast, Silent Autofocus
  • L-Series Weather Sealing (Dust and Moisture Resistant)
  • Fluorine Coating on Front and Rear Elements
  • Customizable Control Ring for Exposure Adjustment
  • Focus/Control Ring Switch on Barrel
  • Compatible with Canon RF Mount Cameras
  • 9-Blade Circular Aperture for Beautiful Bokeh
  • Nano USM Focus Motor for Video AF
  • Minimum Focus Distance: 21cm (Wide) / 38cm (Tele)
