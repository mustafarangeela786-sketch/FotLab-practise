Product Description
The Manfrotto MT055CXPRO3 is a professional-grade carbon fiber tripod engineered for photographers who demand precision, strength, and portability. Constructed from high-modulus carbon fiber, it offers an exceptional strength-to-weight ratio — sturdy enough to hold professional camera systems, yet light enough for travel. Its signature Easy Link attachment and 90-degree column system allow the horizontal centre column to be repositioned, enabling macro and low-angle shooting from unique perspectives. The three independent leg angle selectors provide maximum stability on uneven terrain, making it as useful in the studio as it is in the field.
Key Features
  • Carbon Fiber Construction — Lightweight and Strong
  • 90-Degree Horizontal Centre Column System
  • Easy Link Connector for Accessories (Arm, Reflector, etc.)
  • 3 Independent Leg Angle Selections (24°, 55°, 87°)
  • Quick Power Lock (QPL) Leg Locks for Fast Setup
  • Anti-Rotation System on Centre Column
  • Retractable Rubber / Metal Spike Feet
  • 1/4" and 3/8" Threaded Connections on Top Plate
  • Bubble Level for Perfect Horizontal Alignment
  • Column Can Invert for Ground-Level Macro Shooting
  • Compatible with Most Ball Heads and Pan-Tilt Heads
  • Head Not Included (Sold Separately)
