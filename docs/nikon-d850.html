Product Description
The Nikon D850 is widely regarded as the most capable DSLR camera ever produced. Featuring a back-side illuminated 45.7-megapixel full-frame sensor — with no optical low-pass filter for maximum sharpness — the D850 delivers resolution that rivals medium format cameras. Designed to satisfy the needs of landscape photographers, commercial studio shooters, and wildlife photographers, it combines extraordinary image quality with a robust and reliable body. Its 4K UHD video, fast 9fps burst rate (with battery grip), and class-leading dynamic range make it the definitive professional DSLR for demanding applications.
Key Features
  • 45.7MP Full-Frame BSI CMOS Sensor (No OLPF)
  • 4K UHD Video Recording at 30fps
  • Multi-CAM 20K AF System (153 AF Points)
  • 9fps Continuous Shooting (7fps Native)
  • ISO 64 Base — Outstanding Low Light Performance
  • 3.2-inch Tilting Touchscreen (2.36M Dots)
  • Dual Card Slots (XQD + SD UHS-II)
  • Focus Shift Shooting for Focus Stacking
  • Built-in Time-Lapse and Interval Timer
  • Illuminated Buttons for Low-Light Shooting
  • Silent Photography via Electronic Front Curtain
  • Weather Sealed Magnesium Alloy Body
