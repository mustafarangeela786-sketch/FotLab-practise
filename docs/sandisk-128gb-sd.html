Product Description
The SanDisk Extreme Pro 128GB SDXC UHS-I memory card is built for professional photographers and videographers who cannot afford to miss a shot. With read speeds up to 200MB/s and write speeds up to 140MB/s, it supports continuous burst photography with high-resolution cameras and smooth 4K UHD and 8K video recording. Designed to withstand extreme conditions — it is temperature-proof, waterproof, shockproof, and X-ray-proof — it is the ideal companion for professional use in any environment. The card comes bundled with RescuePRO Deluxe data recovery software for added peace of mind.

  • UHS Speed Grade 3 (U3) and Video Speed Class 30 (V30)
